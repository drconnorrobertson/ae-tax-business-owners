How This Site Is Organized

Business owners rarely have a single tax question. The entity you choose affects how you pay yourself, how you pay yourself affects the retirement contributions you can make, and all of it interacts with the deductions your business claims. The guides here are grouped into hubs so you can follow one thread from start to finish or move across topics as your situation changes.

Each guide explains the concept, walks through simplified examples, points out common mistakes, and lists questions worth raising with your advisor. Tax rules are hedged where they depend on facts, and every page carries a short educational disclaimer.

Who These Guides Are For

The content is written for owner-operators, including sole proprietors, single-member LLC owners, S corporation shareholders, partners, and closely held C corporation owners. It assumes you already have a profitable or growing business and want to understand your options before making decisions. It does not assume you have a finance background.
