Tax Deductions Every Business Owner Misses

By AE Tax Advisors Team | September 2026

Most business owners know about obvious deductions. But the ones that save the most go unclaimed year after year.

1. Home Office

Dedicated space used regularly and exclusively for business qualifies. The actual expense method can yield $3,000 to $5,000 annually.

2. Vehicle Expenses

Track business miles for client visits, bank trips, and supply runs. A mileage app captures thousands in deductions.

3. Meals with Clients

50% deductible when business is discussed. Note attendees and topics on the receipt.

4. Health Insurance

100% deductible above-the-line for self-employed owners, spouses, and dependents. A $18,000 family plan is a huge deduction.

5. Retirement Contributions

A Solo 401(k) allows up to $69,000 in 2026. At $300K income and 35% bracket, that saves over $24,000 in federal taxes.

6. Cell Phone and Internet

Deduct the business-use percentage. 75% business use on a $150/mo phone bill = $1,350/r deduction.

7. Education and Professional Development

Courses, certifications, conferences, and books that improve skills in your current business are deductible.

8. Augusta Rule (Section 280A(g))

Rent your home to your business for up to 14 days tax-free. Fair market rates of $500 to $1,500/day could generate $7,000 to $21,000 in tax-free income with a matching business deduction.

Combined, these deductions can reduce taxable income by $30,000 to $80,000 per year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Augusta Rule?

IRC 280A(g) lets you rent your home to your business for up to 14 days tax-free.

Can I deduct my cell phone bill?

Yes, the business-use percentage of your cell phone bill is deductible.

What is the max home office deduction?

$1,500 simplified or unlimited via actual expense method.
