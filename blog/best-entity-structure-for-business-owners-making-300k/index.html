Best Entity Structure for Business Owners Making $300K

By AE Tax Advisors Team | September 2026

Choosing the right entity structure is one of the highest-impact tax decisions a business owner can make. At $300,000 in net income, the difference between the wrong structure and the right one can cost you $15,000 to $25,000 per year.

Option 1: Single-Member LLC

A single-member LLC is simple but expensive at $300K. All income faces self-employment tax of approximately $33,922.

Option 2: S-Corp Election

At $300K with a $120,000 salary, an S-Corp saves approximately $15,000 to $18,000 per year in self-employment taxes compared to a default LLC.

Key advantages: $180,000 in distributions avoids FICA, QBI deduction applies, and no entity-level tax.

Option 3: C-Corp

A C-Corp pays 21% flat corporate tax. At $300K net income, corporate tax would be $63,000. Double taxation on dividends makes this more expensive unless retaining earnings or planning a QSBS exit.

The Verdict

For most $300K business owners, the S-Corp election is the clear winner. A detailed tax projection comparing all three structures is the best way to decide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an S-Corp or C-Corp better at $300K?

For most service-based business owners, an S-Corp is better. It eliminates SE tax on distributions without double taxation.

Can I lose the QBI deduction at $300K?

Yes, QBI phases out for SSTBs above $191,950 (single) or $383,900 (joint).

Should I have both an S-Corp and C-Corp?

Some owners benefit from a dual-entity structure but it requires legitimate business purpose.
