Why Amendment Recovery Matters

Tax returns are filed under pressure. Deadlines loom, information arrives late, and many preparers focus on accuracy and compliance rather than optimization. The result is that millions of business owners file returns every year that are correct but not optimal. Deductions are missed, credits go unclaimed, depreciation is calculated using less favorable methods, and entity elections that could save thousands are never made.

The good news is that the IRS allows you to correct these mistakes. By filing amended returns, you can recover overpaid taxes from prior years. For many of our clients, the amendment review is the first step in our engagement, and it frequently produces immediate refunds of $5,000 to $50,000 or more before any forward-looking planning begins.
