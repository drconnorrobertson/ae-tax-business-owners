Business Tax Planning Workbooks

Turn a planning question into a file your bookkeeper, preparer and advisor can use. These workbooks organize decisions and records; they do not calculate individual tax results.

  1. Quarterly Tax Cash Flow Workbook for Business Owners

    Build a quarterly planning file that separates estimated tax, withholding, cash reserves and the final tax projection.

  2. Entity Election Readiness File Before an S Corp Decision

    Organize ownership, payroll costs and implementation responsibilities before evaluating an S corporation election.

  3. Owner Pay Reconciliation: Payroll, Draws and Loans

    Use a monthly reconciliation to distinguish wages, distributions, reimbursements and shareholder loan activity.

  4. Retirement Plan Census Workbook for Growing Businesses

    Prepare employee, ownership and cash-flow information before comparing business retirement plan proposals.

  5. Business Tax Decision Register: From Idea to Filing

    Track tax planning assumptions, evidence, responsibilities and reporting steps in one decision register.

  6. First Tax Advisory Meeting Workbook for Business Owners

    Prepare a focused first meeting with AE Tax Advisors and turn the discussion into an agreed scope and action list.
