Engineering firms: Entity election readiness

Document the operating facts before evaluating an election. Record project delivery, technical review, management and the mix of owner and employee services.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Prepare ownership, current classification, duties, profit expectations and recurring compliance costs. Identify deadlines and work already completed. An interactive preparation file does not decide eligibility or submit an election.

Record project delivery, technical review, management and the mix of owner and employee services.

A situation to work through

An engineering owner moves from technical delivery to reviewing work performed by staff.

For this situation, start with project ledger. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Preserve time and responsibility evidence that reflects the current role.
