Ecommerce businesses: Quarterly projection inputs

Prepare a current operating and household input file. Separate marketplace receipts, inventory, sales-tax collections, returns and platform fees.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Use one reporting cutoff. Separate recurring activity from proposed transactions and reconcile withholding and estimated payments to evidence. Label forecasts and unresolved balances so the advisor can compare the updated picture with the prior projection.

A situation to work through

A seller's platform payout differs substantially from reported gross sales.

For this situation, start with marketplace exports. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Build a bridge from gross activity to net deposits rather than equating them.
