Multi-owner businesses: Retirement plan census

Build an accurate employee and compensation file for a plan discussion. Document ownership, agreed distributions, capital contributions and decision authority.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Record the census date, employee categories, compensation source and expected changes. Keep private identifiers out of this public worksheet. Ask the administrator which additional records are needed for a design and funding comparison.

Document ownership, agreed distributions, capital contributions and decision authority.

A situation to work through

Two owners contribute different amounts and draw cash at different times.

For this situation, start with operating agreement. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Ask the preparer to reconcile ownership and ledger balances before drawing tax conclusions.

Question for the reviewer

Expected handoff

A census-source register and provider responsibility map.

Separate facts supported by documents from estimates and unresolved assumptions. Assign each missing item to a responsible person and a date. Send the reconciliation alongside the source references so the reviewer can trace each conclusion.
