# Imported founder identity corrections

A review of imported founder descriptions against company founding dates found two unrelated historical identities. Date-pattern matches were treated only as candidates: sports seasons and terms in office are not birth/death dates, and successor corporations may retain predecessor history.

TechSmith's imported record points to William Rowan Hamilton (1805–1865), the mathematician. TechSmith's own about page identifies Bill Hamilton as its founder and states that the business was founded in 1987. The Michigan State University Research Foundation's December 19, 2024 remembrance independently identifies its graduate Bill Hamilton and his founding of TechSmith in 1987. Use the sourced name without inventing a Wikidata identity or a new person biography.

- https://www.techsmith.com/about/
- https://msufoundation.org/remembering-bill-hamilton/

Hasbro Entertainment's imported record points to Gabriel Marcel (1889–1973), the philosopher. Hasbro's August 16, 2023 announcement instead describes its new entertainment division and appoints Olivier Dumont as President, Zev Foreman as Head of Film and Gabriel Marano as Head of Television. These leadership roles must not be relabeled as founder roles. The corrected company page reports the dated division history and source; no individual founder is asserted.

- https://newsroom.hasbro.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hasbro-announces-leadership-team-new-entertainment-division

Remove the two incorrect person associations from active founder search and indexable sitemaps. Preserve both old URLs as noindex correction notices linking to their respective corrected company pages. Preserve the already released SeaWorld correction. Add regression checks against reintroduction of either false identity and against labeling Hasbro's appointed executives as founders.
